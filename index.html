A LITTLE ABOUT ME

I've never believed you have to be just one thing.

People can hold many interests and find meaning in work that doesn't fit neatly into one box.

Over the past 5+ years, my work has spanned business operations, programme delivery, brand strategy and project coordination, alongside an MBA in Operations Management. Each role showed me a different side of how businesses run, and I kept coming back to the same questions: What's really happening here? Why isn't it working? What would make it better?

That's what I love about operations. It lets me connect the dots, work with all kinds of people, and get into the details without losing sight of the bigger picture.

When I'm not working, you'll probably find me lost in a sci-fi book or film, halfway through a thriller I can't put down, or, every now and then, curled up with a good romance.
